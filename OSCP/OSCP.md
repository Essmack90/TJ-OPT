# OSCP Vault

This is the map for the vault. If you are new, follow the runbook before opening individual command pages.

## Beginner route

1. [[RUNBOOK V2/Index|RUNBOOK V2 index]]: understand the workflow and choose a branch.
2. [[RUNBOOK V2/00 - Follow-Along Controller|Follow-Along Controller]]: run a box from setup through closeout.
3. [[BOXES/MASTER BOX LIST|Master Box List]]: choose a machine and record its status.
4. [[REFERENCE CARDS/FAQ - Quick Answers|FAQ and Quick Answers]]: recover when a command or finding is unfamiliar.
5. [[REFERENCE CARDS/Box Report Template|Box Report Template]]: turn the evidence into a beginner-readable write-up.
6. [[OSCP COMMAND MASTER CHEATSHEET|Command Master Cheatsheet]]: look up syntax after the runbook tells you what to do.

## Exam route

Use [[OSCP/EXAM RUNBOOK/Index|OSCP Exam Runbook]] when time is the constraint. It is the stripped-down command and branching companion to RUNBOOK V2 and is updated from every completed write-up.

## How the folders fit together

- **RUNBOOK V2** is the operational path to follow during a box.
- **MODULES** explains the underlying concepts and exam objectives.
- **COMMAND APPENDIX** is the syntax-first reference once you know what to try.
- **DECISION TREE** and **COMMAND BREAKDOWNS** explain how to choose and troubleshoot a technique.
- **BOXES/WRITE UPS** contains completed machine notes. Read the reconnaissance and decision points, not just the final payload.
