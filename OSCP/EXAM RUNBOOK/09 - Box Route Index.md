## How to use it under time pressure

1. Recon the box and choose the OS or AD branch first.
2. Open the matching write-up only when a service, application, or permission finding matches the evidence.
3. Translate its successful route into the variables and evidence paths in the exam pages.
4. Add a new box here and to the relevant fast-path page after the run is complete.
